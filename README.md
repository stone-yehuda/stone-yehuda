I sit where **data, AI, and go-to-market** meet. I design and ship production AI agents and automation pipelines for sales and revenue teams, then measure whether they actually move pipeline. 6+ years in data and analytics before moving into applied AI.

Most of my day-job code lives in private company repos, so this profile is where I share the patterns, side projects, and experiments I can make public.

### 🛠️ What I build

- **Agentic prospecting systems**: multi-step LangGraph agents that research accounts, personalize outreach, and route to humans for review-and-send
- **CRM automation**: agents that turn call transcripts into clean CRM updates, deal hygiene, and next steps
- **GTM data pipelines**: enrichment, scoring, and syncing across CRM, data warehouse, and outreach tools
- **Evaluation & observability**: tracing, datasets, and evals so agents stay reliable in production
- **Analytics**: SQL/dbt models and BI dashboards that tie AI work back to revenue metrics

### 🎮 Passion project: Worldforge

An original science-fantasy RTS I'm building in **Godot 4 / GDScript**, plus the world and a novel set inside it.

- 4 asymmetric factions, 65 unit chassis across 3 tiers, heroes, tech trees, and siege
- Squad formations, an armor/ward combat model, knockback physics, and a 30 Hz sim with physics interpolation
- An enemy AI that runs its own economy, waves, and targeting, plus headless sim tooling to balance matches AI vs. AI
- Built with a **multi-agent dev workflow**: Claude Code, Codex, and Gemini CLI working one repo under shared rules, a task backlog, and an honest "built vs. seen working" state table
