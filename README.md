I work where **data, AI, and go-to-market** meet: production AI agents and automation for sales and revenue teams, measured on whether they move pipeline. 6+ years in data and analytics before applied AI. Most of my day-job code is private, so this is where I share what I can.

### What I build

- **Agentic prospecting**: LangGraph agents that research accounts, personalize outreach, and hand off to humans for review-and-send
- **CRM automation**: call transcripts turned into clean CRM updates and next steps
- **GTM data pipelines**: enrichment, scoring, and sync across CRM, warehouse, and outreach tools
- **Evals & observability**: tracing, datasets, and evals that keep agents reliable in production

### Passion project: Worldforge 🎮

An original science-fantasy RTS in **Godot 4 / GDScript**, plus the world and a novel set inside it.

- 4 asymmetric factions, 65 unit chassis across 3 tiers, heroes, tech trees, and siege
- Squad formations, an armor/ward combat model, knockback physics, and a 30 Hz sim
- Enemy AI with its own economy and waves, plus headless AI-vs-AI balance tooling
- Built with a **multi-agent workflow**: Claude Code, Codex, and Gemini CLI on one repo under shared rules
